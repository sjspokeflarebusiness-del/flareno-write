Yes. For custom orders, I include one round of small revisions (like fixing a sentence or adding a paragraph). Bigger changes may cost extra.

For a 500-word article, I can usually deliver within 24 hours. For longer or research-heavy articles (1000+ words), I need 2 to 3 days.
